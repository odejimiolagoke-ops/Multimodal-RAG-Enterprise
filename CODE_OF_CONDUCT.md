# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.
Participants should be considerate of differing perspectives and experiences,
and should focus discussion on ideas and project outcomes.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
threats, deliberate intimidation, and publishing another person's private
information without permission.

## Scope

This standard applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other project-related communications.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer using the project's
designated contact channel. Do not post sensitive information in a public
issue. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality as
far as practical, and take proportionate action. This may include a warning,
content removal, temporary restrictions, or removal from project spaces.

Maintainers are expected to apply these rules consistently and to explain
enforcement decisions when doing so does not compromise someone's privacy or
safety.
